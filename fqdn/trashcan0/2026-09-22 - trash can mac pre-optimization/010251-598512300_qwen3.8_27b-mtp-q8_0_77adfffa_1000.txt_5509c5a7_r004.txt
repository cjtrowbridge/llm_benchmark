## Summary

The story follows Mara as she wanders through an old town. Her walk is set in motion by a traveler's mention of a distant bridge, which reminds her of a letter she never sent. She strolls past a bakery, listens to a clock, and visits a market where a mapmaker describes a winding road beyond an orchard. She pauses for tea, watches clouds, and is pulled back into a memory of a rainy train ride from years ago. By evening, she takes a different lane home, taking in lamps, bicycles, and the smell of fresh bread. The narrative then loops back, repeating many of the same details, giving the story a circular, meditative quality.
