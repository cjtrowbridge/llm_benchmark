## Summary

The story is highly repetitive, cycling through the same handful of scenes in various orders. In essence, a woman named **Mara** strolls leisurely through an old town. She passes a bakery, listens to a clock, stops for tea, watches clouds, and remembers a rainy train ride from years ago. A traveler tells her about a distant bridge, which makes her recall a letter she once meant to send. At the market, a mapmaker describes a winding road beyond the orchard, and Mara asks where it ends. By evening she returns by a different lane, noticing lamps, bicycles, and the smell of fresh bread. These same few moments are restated over and over throughout the passage.

Buried among the repeated sentences is one distinct, unusual line: *"The brass key was gold."*

## Answer to the final question

The brass key was **gold**.
