## Summary

The story is a heavily repetitive, almost dreamlike passage about a woman named **Mara** who wanders through an old town with no particular hurry. Over and over, the same few scenes recur in shuffled order: she passes a bakery, listens to a clock, stops for tea while watching clouds cross a square, and remembers a rainy train ride from years ago. A traveler tells her about a distant bridge, prompting Mara to recall a letter she meant to send. At the market, a mapmaker describes a winding road beyond the orchard, and Mara asks where it ends. By evening, she returns home by a different lane, taking in lamps, bicycles, and the smell of fresh bread. The entire narrative cycles through these quiet, sensory moments again and again, creating a meditative, circular atmosphere.
