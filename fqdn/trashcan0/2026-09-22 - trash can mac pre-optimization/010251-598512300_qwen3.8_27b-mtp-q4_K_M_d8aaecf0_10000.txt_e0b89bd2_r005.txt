## Summary

Mara walks through an old town in no particular hurry. Along the way, she passes a bakery and listens to a clock, stops for tea while watching clouds drift across a square (which triggers a memory of a rainy train ride from years ago), listens to a traveler describe a distant bridge (reminding her of a letter she never sent), and visits a market where a mapmaker tells her about a winding road beyond an orchard. She asks the mapmaker where the road ends. By evening, she returns home by a different lane, taking in the glow of lamps, the sight of bicycles, and the smell of fresh bread.

Notably, the story consists of these same handful of scenes **repeated dozens of times** in slightly different orders, creating a circular, looping narrative with no real progression.

## Answer

The brass key was **gold**. (This detail appears exactly once amid the many repetitions: *"The brass key was gold."*)
